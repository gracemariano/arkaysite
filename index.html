Section 02

Vision & Principles

10-year vision

Professionally governed, multi-generational real estate platform where family contributes as executives, directors, or passive owners — not by default entitlement. Brand and lender relationships remain as strong as under the founding generation.

Guiding principles

  • Merit before title — roles via performance and external benchmarks
  • One voice to the market — unified messaging on material deals
  • Transparency among owners — quarterly reporting to all equity holders
  • Preserve optionality — no forced sale of core income assets for estate tax without plan

Section 05

Governance Structure

Family council

  • Members: Michell, Josh, Max (+ spouses when equity affected)
  • Quorum: 3 of 3 principals; Walter attends as non-voting secretary
  • Quarterly meetings; extraordinary session within 10 days for deals > $5M
  • Minutes in secure data room — 7-year retention

Operating board

  • Independent directors: 1 by Year 2, 2 by Year 4
  • Audit & compensation committees before Michell relinquishes chair
  • Walter coordinates charter, bylaws, shareholder agreements

Dispute resolution

(1) 48-hour principal discussion → (2) Walter-facilitated mediation → (3) binding arbitration (Ontario) for operational deadlocks. Ownership disputes: supermajority per draft charter.

Employment vs. ownership

Non-employed shareholders receive distributions per class rights only. Employment termination does not auto-trigger buyout; formula in separate agreement.

Section 06

Ownership & Estate Alignment

Current state (confirm with counsel)

  • Holdco / opco stack with multiple property SPVs
  • Voting control concentrated — economic interests may differ
  • Insurance & trusts last reviewed 2019 — refresh required

Target structure

  • Voting trust for unified control during transition
  • Gradual economic transfer (estate freeze or equivalent) — tax model by Q4
  • Key-person insurance aligned to debt covenants
  • Tag-along / drag-along for third-party sales

Buy-sell triggers (draft)

Death, disability, voluntary exit, termination for cause, or deadlock > 90 days. Valuation: independent appraiser + EBITDA multiple floor/ceiling. First refusal: family → company → third party. Walter to circulate markup within 30 days.

Section 09

Financial Guardrails

Leverage & liquidity

  • Portfolio LTV: covenant max minus 5% buffer
  • Minimum liquidity: 6 months G&A + known capex
  • No new personal guarantees from next generation without council approval

Compensation

  • Market benchmarks (REIT / private peer set)
  • LTI tied to NAV & development margin — 3-year cliff
  • Michell transition comp documented separately

Distributions

Sustainable payout after reserves, debt service, and development funding. Non-employed shareholders: predictable quarterly formula in charter.

Reporting

Monthly: cash, covenants. Quarterly: full board pack to council. Annual: audited financials + independent valuation summary.
